One block under a whole moon.

Cubelune is a small Minecraft server. You start on one block floating in the sky, break it, and it comes back as something else. Your island grows out of that. Minions keep working while you're off doing something else, and Java and Bedrock players share the same world.

It runs on a machine at home, so it restarts now and then when things get added. You get a warning in chat first.

What you actually do

  • Break the block. Dirt, logs, ores, chests, the odd mob. Twenty phases, from Plains all the way down to the Sulfur Caves. The first full pass is 15,500 blocks.
  • Sell and buy. /shop on your island. Coins buy minions, spawners and gear.
  • Minions. Miner, Farmer, Lumberjack, Fisher, Slayer, Collector, Feeder, Seller. They run while you're online anywhere on the server. To keep them going after you log off you need fuel cells, which come from prestiges, the block itself, crates or Moonstones. The Miner stays locked until you've done one full pass yourself, otherwise it would play the game for you.
  • Prestige. When the pass counter fills, reset the island and go up one moon rank. Each pass is 20% longer than the last, and you can prestige once a day.
  • Moonstones. 10,000 coins become one Moonstone, one way only. /moonstones turns them into crate keys, fuel cells and permanent island upgrades: Sell Boost, Fuel Tank, Fuel Efficiency, Island Size and Co-op Size. Upgrades survive resets.
  • Compacting. Nine diamond blocks make a Super Diamond, nine of those a Mega, then Ultra. Same for netherite. The tiers turn into armor with real stat bonuses and set perks. Compacted blocks can't be placed; right-click one to split it back.
  • Prismatic. The tier above Ultra Netherite. Breaking the one block by hand can drop a Prismatic Shard, about one in 250,000 (minions never find one). In /prismatic, Ultra Netherite plus one shard makes a piece of armor or a tool, once you have 200 hours on the server. Nine shards make a Prismatic Block you can place, worth about 250 island levels.
  • Tools that help. Every pickaxe smelts the ores it mines, and whatever a pickaxe, axe, shovel or hoe breaks goes straight into your inventory.
  • Enchants, crates, cosmetics. Over a hundred custom enchants at the Enchanter, paid in XP. Three crates in the hub. Pets, mounts, morphs, particle effects, gadgets and more in /cosmetics, paid for with Cosmetic Tokens from the crates.

Rules

  • No cheating. No hacked clients, macros or dupes. Every block is logged, so griefing can always be rolled back.
  • Don't wreck islands you were invited to. Griefing a co-op gets rolled back and you get removed.
  • Be decent in chat. No spam, no ads, no slurs. Staff have a tag next to their name. Anyone without one claiming to be staff isn't.

FAQ

Is it pay to win?
No. Nothing is for sale. If that ever changes it will be cosmetics or convenience, and it will be written here first.
Why was the server down?
It runs at home. Restarts happen when something new goes in. Everyone gets a warning in chat and is kicked just before, so inventories save properly.
My block or island is gone.
Say so. The world is backed up automatically, and the one block puts itself back if it disappears.
Which versions work?
Java 26.2 and newer. Bedrock on the current version, from phone, console or Windows.
Can I share an island with friends?
Yes, five people to start and up to eight with the Co-op Size upgrade. /ob team invite <name> on your island.
It asked me to download a resource pack.
That's the Cubelune pack, about 17 KB, and it only holds the Prismatic textures. Without it Prismatic items look like plain amethyst and netherite. Bedrock doesn't get the pack yet, so the items look plain there.
How do I get back to the hub?
/spawn. /ob takes you home again.
