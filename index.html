A OneBlock server you can play from your phone.

Cubelune is a small Minecraft server. You start on one block floating in the sky, break it, and it comes back as something else. Your island grows out of that. Minions keep working on it while you're off doing something else, and Java and Bedrock players share the same world.

It runs on a machine at home, so it restarts now and then when things get added. You get a warning in chat first.

What you actually do

  • Break the block. Dirt, logs, ores, chests, the odd mob. Twenty phases, from Plains all the way down to the Sulfur Caves. The first full pass is 15,500 blocks.
  • Sell and buy. /shop on your island. Coins buy minions, spawners and gear.
  • Minions. Miner, Farmer, Lumberjack, Fisher, Slayer, Collector, Feeder, Seller. They run while you're online anywhere on the server. To keep them going after you log off you need fuel cells, which come from prestiges, the block itself, crates or Moonstones. The Miner stays locked until you've done one full pass yourself, otherwise it would play the game for you.
  • Prestige. When the pass counter fills, reset the island and go up one moon rank. Each pass is 20% longer than the last, and you can prestige once a day.
  • Moonstones. 10,000 coins become one Moonstone, one way only. They buy permanent island upgrades: sell boost, bigger fuel tank, larger island, bigger team. Those survive resets.
  • Compacting. Nine diamond blocks make a Super Diamond, nine of those a Mega, then Ultra. Same for netherite. The tiers turn into armour with real stat bonuses and set perks.
  • Enchants, crates, spawners. Over a hundred custom enchants at the Enchanter, paid in XP. Stardust, Lunar and Nova crates in the hub. Spawners stack up to 50 in a single block, and which mobs you can buy depends on your rank.

Player stats

Type a name and you get what the in-game sidebar shows for that player: rank, money, networth, island level, Moonstones, fuel and pass progress. Updated about once a minute. Bedrock names work with or without the dot.

Rules

  • No cheating. No hacked clients, macros or dupes. There's an anti-cheat and every block is logged, so it's not worth it.
  • Don't wreck islands you were invited to. Griefing a co-op gets rolled back and you get removed.
  • Be decent in chat. No spam, no ads, no slurs. Staff have a tag next to their name. Anyone without one claiming to be staff isn't.

FAQ

Is it pay to win?
No. Nothing is for sale. If that ever changes it will be cosmetics or convenience, and it will be written here first.
Why was the server down?
It runs at home. Restarts happen when something new goes in. Everyone gets a warning in chat and is kicked just before, so inventories save properly.
My block or island is gone.
Say so. Backups run every few hours and the one block puts itself back if it disappears.
Which versions work?
Java 26.2 and newer. Bedrock on the current version, from phone, console or Windows.
Can I share an island with friends?
Yes, up to five people. /ob team invite <name> on your island.
How do I get back to the hub?
/spawn. /ob takes you home again.
