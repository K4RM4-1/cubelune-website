How it works

Everything on Cubelune loops back to the one block. Here's what's waiting once you spawn on your island.

▣The one block

Break it and it comes back as something new. 20 phases from Plains to the Sulfur Caves, 15,500 blocks per full pass, with loot chests and mobs along the way.

⚙Minions

Eight minion types farm, mine, fish and sell for you while you're online elsewhere. Fuel cells keep your island running after you log off. The Miner unlocks after your first full pass.

☾Prestige

Finish a pass, reset your island, and climb the moon ranks: New Moon to Supermoon. Each rank adds minion slots, homes and a bigger fuel tank, plus a coin bonus every time.

✦Moonstones & upgrades

Burn 10,000 coins for a Moonstone and spend them on permanent island upgrades: sell boost, fuel efficiency, island size, co-op size. Upgrades survive prestige resets.

⬢Compacting & gear

Nine diamond blocks make a Super Diamond, nine of those a Mega, then Ultra. Same for netherite. Forge tier armor with real stat bonuses and full-set perks.

◈Crates & enchants

Stardust, Lunar and Nova crates in the hub, with keys from the one block, prestiges and Moonstones. Over a hundred custom enchantments at the Enchanter, bought with XP.

Getting started

  1. Add the server with the address above. On Bedrock, remember the port.
  2. Spawn in the hub and tap the OneBlock NPC, or type /ob. Your island is built in a few seconds.
  3. Break the block. Sell drops with /shop on your island, check your money and rank on the sidebar.
  4. Automate with minions from the shop, then prestige when the counter hits 15,500. /spawn always brings you back to the hub.

Rules

No cheating

No hacked clients, macros or exploits. The server watches for it, and dupes are patched fast.

Respect co-ops

Islands are shared trust. Griefing or stealing from a co-op you were invited to gets you rolled back and removed.

Keep chat clean

Be decent to people. No spam, no ads, no slurs. Staff are marked with a tag and can help.
